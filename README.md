# SMoRG's QoL

**Hear your quest progress:** *"Work, work."* when you finish an objective, *"Work complete."* when the quest is done. Prefer the Alliance? Switch to the Human worker (*"More work?"* / *"Job's done!"*).

On top of the quest sounds come a bunch of small quality-of-life tweaks, each one **individually toggleable**. Works in Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, Anniversary and Mists of Pandaria Classic.

## What's new in 1.8

- 🟦 **Square minimap** — a square minimap with a thin black border in place of Blizzard's round frame. Addon minimap buttons follow the square edge, and in WoW Forever the day/night indicator sits in the top right corner. Retail and WoW Forever.

It is off by default; turn it on under **Interface** or with `/sqol sqm`. See the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) for the full release history.

## Features

**Quests**
- 🔊 **Quest sounds** — Peon or Human worker voice lines when you finish an objective or a whole quest.
- 🤖 **Quest automation** — accepts regular, daily and weekly quests and turns in completed ones. Never picks a reward for you and never hands in gold, currency or non-quest items.
- 📡 **Smart Quest Radar** — a quest icon and objective counts (e.g. `2/8 · 1/5`) above the nameplates of units you still need.
- ⭐ **New WoW Forever quests** — a blue star on quests that are new in WoW Forever, in quest giver lists, the quest text, the quest log, the objective tracker and on the world map.
- 🏷️ **Nameplate objective counts** — quest progress above relevant nameplates.
- 🧭 **Auto-track new quests** in the objective tracker.
- 🎨 **Colored quest progress** — counts like `3/10` go from red through yellow to green, in chat and in the quest tracker.
- 📖 **Adventure Recap** — a window with this session's quests, reputation, playtime and top faction.

**Character, reputation & XP**
- 🧾 **Item level and speed** on your player frame.
- 🎨 **Colored XP/reputation numbers**, plus percentage left and standing on the reputation bar.
- 🤝 **Auto-watch reputation** — switches to the faction you just gained reputation with.
- 🧠 **Reputation Memory** — returns to your preferred faction 10 seconds later.
- ✨ **Floating reputation gains** — `+25 Rep — Valarjar` near the screen center.
- 📈 **Floating skill gains** — `+1 Staves (18/20)` the same way when a profession or weapon skill goes up.

**Group**
- ⏱️ **Ready check countdown** and ⏳ **LFG queue pop countdown**.
- 👥 **Party member levels** on the party frames.

**Interface**
- 🎯 **Target in unit tooltips** — who the unit is targeting, with a red `You` when it is you.
- 🎨 **Colorful unit tooltips**.
- 🔗 **Wowhead links** and the **Show web link** key binding.
- 🏹 **Range icon and distance** to your target on its nameplate.
- 🟦 **Square minimap** with a thin black border; addon minimap buttons follow the square edge.
- ✅ **Hide completed achievements** by default.
- 🖋️ **Custom damage text font** and 🖱️ **cursor shake highlight**.

## Classic clients

The quest sounds and most features work everywhere. A few need Retail-only parts of the UI and are simply left out of the options in the Classic clients: Smart Quest Radar, nameplate objective counts, auto-track, the range icon, the square minimap and the colored counts in the quest tracker. Classic Era and Anniversary also have no achievements or group finder, so those two features are only in Mists of Pandaria Classic.

## Settings

Open the options window from:
- 🧩 a **LibDataBroker display** such as Bazooka or Titan Panel,
- 📋 the **addon compartment** menu by the minimap,
- 🗺️ the optional **minimap button** (`/sqol minimap`), or
- 💬 `/sqol config`.

**Esc → Options → AddOns → SMoRG's QoL** has a button that opens the same window, and every option has a chat command. Type `/sqol help` for the full list.

## Translations

The addon follows your game language: English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The voice lines are always in English.

⚠️ The translations have not been checked by native speakers yet. If something reads oddly, please suggest a better wording on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/smorgsqol) or [GitHub](https://github.com/SMoRG75/SMoRGsQoL/issues).
