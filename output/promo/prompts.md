# Promotional image prompts

Prompts for an image generation tool. Updated for version 1.1.0: the art
keeps the navy, antique gold, turquoise and parchment fantasy look of the
earlier images, but shows the 1.1.0 features and both supported clients.

Save the results as `smorgsqol-banner.png` (about 16:9, e.g. 1672 x 941) and
`smorgsqol-social.png` (square, e.g. 1254 x 1254) in this folder, replacing
the old images. Keep each PNG below 2,000,000 bytes for CurseForge; optimize
after generation without reducing text readability.

Check each result before using it: all text must be spelled exactly as in the
prompt, with no extra words, logos or garbled lettering.

## Banner

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished wide promotional banner for a World of Warcraft addon that supports both Retail and WoW Forever. Brand title verbatim: "SMoRG's QoL". Large tagline verbatim: "Hear objectives complete. Hear quests complete." Other text, exactly four feature captions from left to right: "Quest objective completion sound", "Quest completion sound", "Know your range", "Every setting in one window". Footer text: "Retail & WoW Forever · Every feature is toggleable" and small "Available on CurseForge". Rich illustrated fantasy aesthetic, dark navy backdrop, warm antique gold title, tasteful turquoise magical glows, subtle parchment and metal details. Strong readable typography and generous spacing. Use four beautifully illustrated conceptual symbols, one above each caption: a quest objective scroll with unfinished 0/10, 3/10 and 7/10 rows and a green checked 10/10 row beside a ringing bell; a separate whole completed quest scroll with a large green check, gold seal and ringing bell; a red crosshair reticle inside a gold ring with a white arrow breaking out toward the upper right; an ornate open settings panel of parchment and gold with glowing toggle switches. Both sound features must be visually and textually distinct and most prominent. These are conceptual feature illustrations, not fabricated game screenshots. A high quality cohesive marketing layout with atmospheric depth, restrained ornament, clear text readable at small size. No official Blizzard or Warcraft logos, no faction insignia, no extra text, no fake ratings, no performance claims, no sliders or fabricated settings. Landscape aspect ratio approximately 16:9.
## Social image

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a square social media promotional illustration for a World of Warcraft addon called "SMoRG's QoL", for Retail and WoW Forever. Premium fantasy game aesthetic: navy blue, antique gold trim, turquoise magic, atmospheric painted background. Large perfectly readable title at top exactly "SMoRG's QoL". Beneath it exact subtitle "Your adventure. Your settings." Main hero illustration: an ornate open settings panel of parchment and gold with glowing toggle switches at the center; in front of it an unrolled quest scroll whose progress numbers glow from red to green with a luminous green check; beside it a red crosshair reticle in a gold ring with a white arrow breaking out toward the upper right, surrounded by a soft turquoise halo. Strong cohesive composition, lots of breathing room, richly detailed crafted objects. Make the two sound features prominent as separate settings, labeled exactly "Quest objective completion sound" and "Quest completion sound". Other option labels must describe real addon settings from Options.lua; never invent brightness, volume, display sliders, target highlighting, or generic settings. Show readable labels rather than decorative lines. Bottom copy exactly "Hear objectives complete. Hear quests complete." and below it "Every feature is toggleable." Small bottom call to action exactly "Try it on CurseForge". This is conceptual promotional art, not a game screenshot. No additional marketing text, no official logos or faction insignia, no fake ratings. Make readable typography the priority. Square format.
