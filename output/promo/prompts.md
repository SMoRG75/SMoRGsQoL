# Promotional image prompts

Prompts for an image generation tool. Updated for version 1.1.0: the art
keeps the navy, antique gold, turquoise and parchment fantasy look of the
earlier images, but shows the 1.1.0 features and both supported clients.

Save the results as `smorgsqol-banner.png` (about 16:9, e.g. 1672 x 941) and
`smorgsqol-social.png` (square, e.g. 1254 x 1254) in this folder, replacing
the old images.

Check each result before using it: all text must be spelled exactly as in the
prompt, with no extra words, logos or garbled lettering.

## Banner

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished wide promotional banner for a World of Warcraft addon that supports both Retail and WoW Forever. Brand title verbatim: "SMoRG's QoL". Tagline verbatim: "Small tweaks. A smoother World of Warcraft." Other text, exactly four short feature captions: "Quest progress at a glance", "Know your range", "See who they target", "Every setting in one window". Footer text: "Retail & WoW Forever · Every feature is toggleable" and small "Available on CurseForge". Rich illustrated fantasy aesthetic, dark navy backdrop, warm antique gold title, tasteful turquoise magical glows, subtle parchment and metal details. Strong readable typography and generous spacing. Use four beautifully illustrated symbols, one above each caption: an unrolled quest scroll whose progress numbers glow from red through yellow to green with a green check mark; a red crosshair reticle inside a gold ring with a white arrow breaking out of it toward the upper right; a glowing eye beside a small gold arrow pointing at a target; an ornate open settings panel of parchment and gold with glowing toggle switches. These are conceptual feature illustrations, not fabricated game screenshots. A high quality cohesive marketing layout with atmospheric depth, restrained ornament, clear text readable at small size. No official Blizzard or Warcraft logos, no faction insignia, no extra text, no fake ratings, no performance claims. Landscape aspect ratio approximately 16:9.

## Social image

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a square social media promotional illustration for a World of Warcraft addon called "SMoRG's QoL", for Retail and WoW Forever. Premium fantasy game aesthetic: navy blue, antique gold trim, turquoise magic, atmospheric painted background. Large perfectly readable title at top exactly "SMoRG's QoL". Beneath it exact subtitle "Your adventure. Your settings." Main hero illustration: an ornate open settings panel of parchment and gold with glowing toggle switches at the center; in front of it an unrolled quest scroll whose progress numbers glow from red to green with a luminous green check; beside it a red crosshair reticle in a gold ring with a white arrow breaking out toward the upper right, surrounded by a soft turquoise halo. Strong cohesive composition, lots of breathing room, richly detailed crafted objects. Bottom copy exactly "Track quests. Know your range. See every target." and below it "Every feature is toggleable." Small bottom call to action exactly "Try it on CurseForge". This is conceptual promotional art, not a game screenshot. No additional text, no official logos or faction insignia, no fake ratings. Make readable typography the priority. Square format.
