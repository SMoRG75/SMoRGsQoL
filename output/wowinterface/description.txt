[size=5][b]SMoRG's QoL[/b][/size]

[b]Hear your quest progress:[/b] [i]"Work, work."[/i] when you finish an objective, [i]"Work complete."[/i] when the quest is done. Plus toggleable QoL tweaks for Retail and WoW Forever.

The worker voice lines are where it all started. Prefer the Alliance? Switch to the Human worker ([i]"More work?"[/i] / [i]"Job's done!"[/i]). Every other feature is a small, [b]individually toggleable[/b] tweak.

Everything can be turned on and off from the options window, the in-game Settings UI or [b]/sqol[/b] chat commands.

[size=4][b]Features[/b][/size]

[b]Quest sounds: objective and quest completion[/b]
[list]
[*]Plays a worker voice line when a single objective is completed before the full quest is done (Peon: [i]"Work, work."[/i]).
[*]Plays another voice line and prints a chat message when the whole quest is ready to turn in, or done for bonus and world quests (Peon: [i]"Work complete."[/i]).
[*]Choose the Horde (Peon) or Alliance (Human worker) sound profile, and toggle each sound on its own.
[/list]

[b]Quests[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Auto-track new quests[/b] - newly accepted quests are added to the Objective Tracker.
[*][b]Colored quest progress[/b] - progress like 3/10 or 45% is colored red -> yellow -> green in chat messages and in the quest tracker. Also covers progress-bar objectives and scenario progress bars that the default UI reports silently.
[*][b]Nameplate objective counts[/b] - shows quest progress (e.g. 0/10 or 45%) above the nameplates of quest mobs.
[*][b]Smart Quest Radar[/b] - a quest icon and up to three unfinished objective counts (e.g. 2/8 - 1/5) above the nameplates of units you still need for your quests. It only marks a unit when its tooltip matches your own active quests.
[*][b]Adventure Recap[/b] - a movable window with this session's completed quests, world quests, reputation gained, playtime and your most active faction, kept per character. The previous session stays available too.
[/list]

[b]Character[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Item level and movement speed[/b] on your player frame, each toggled on its own.
[*][b]Range icon and distance[/b] - the distance to your target in yards (e.g. 8-30 yd) on its nameplate, plus a pulsing icon when none of the offensive abilities on your bars can reach it. Works for every class with no spell lists to set up.
[*][b]Target in unit tooltips[/b] - a "Target:" line showing who the unit is targeting, class- or reaction-colored, with a red "You" when it is you.
[/list]

[b]Reputation and XP[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Colored XP/reputation numbers[/b] - the current number on the standard bars goes red -> yellow -> green, and reputation shows the percentage left and your current standing.
[*][b]Auto-watch reputation[/b] - switches your watched faction to the one you just gained reputation with.
[*][b]Reputation Memory[/b] - save a preferred faction per character, and the reputation bar goes back to it 10 seconds after auto-watch has shown a new gain.
[*][b]Floating reputation gains[/b] - a short "+25 Rep - Valarjar" text that floats up and fades out.
[/list]

[b]Group[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Ready check countdown[/b] - the seconds left on a ready check, turning red for the last 5.
[*][b]Queue pop countdown[/b] - how many of your 40 seconds are left to accept a group finder pop.
[*][b]Party member levels[/b] on both default and raid-style party frames.
[/list]

[b]Interface[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Hide completed achievements[/b] - the Achievement UI opens on incomplete achievements (Retail only).
[*][b]Custom damage text font[/b] for floating combat text.
[*][b]Cursor shake highlight[/b] - find your cursor by shaking the mouse.
[*][b]Login splash[/b] showing which features are on.
[/list]


[size=4][b]Options[/b][/size]

All settings are in one movable window, grouped into Quests, Character, Reputation & XP, Group, Interface and Advanced. Open it from:
[list]
[*]LibDataBroker displays such as Bazooka, Titan Panel or ChocolateBar (left-click; right-click opens Blizzard's Settings page)
[*]the addon compartment menu by the minimap
[*]the minimap button (off by default, enable it with [b]/sqol minimap[/b])
[*][b]/sqol config[/b]
[/list]

The same settings are also under [b]Esc -> Options -> AddOns -> SMoRG's QoL[/b], and they stay in sync with the window and the slash commands.

[size=4][b]Slash commands[/b][/size]

Type [b]/sqol[/b] to see the current status, or [b]/sqol help[/b] for the full list.

[code]
/sqol config           open the options window
/sqol minimap   (mm)   minimap button
/sqol questsound (qs)  quest complete sound
/sqol objectivesound (os)  objective complete sound
/sqol soundprofile     Horde or Alliance voice lines
/sqol autotrack (at)   auto-track new quests
/sqol color     (col)  quest progress colors
/sqol barcolor  (bc)   XP/reputation number colors
/sqol nameplate (np)   nameplate objective counts
/sqol radar     (qr)   Smart Quest Radar
/sqol recap            Adventure Recap (last, reset, test)
/sqol ilvl             item level
/sqol speed     (spd)  movement speed
/sqol range     (rng)  range icon and distance
/sqol tooltiptarget (tt)  target in tooltips
/sqol rep       (rw)   auto-watch reputation
/sqol repmemory (rm)   return to preferred reputation
/sqol repfocus         save the watched faction (status, clear)
/sqol reptext   (rt)   floating reputation gains
/sqol readycheck (rc)  ready check countdown
/sqol lfgtimer  (lfg)  queue pop countdown
/sqol partylevel (pl)  party member levels
/sqol hideach   (ha)   hide completed achievements
/sqol damagefont (df)  damage text font
/sqol cursor    (cs)   cursor shake highlight
/sqol splash           login splash
/sqol reset            reset all settings
[/code]


[size=4][b]Translations[/b][/size]

SMoRG's QoL follows your game language: English, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The quest voice lines are always in English, and the slash commands are the same in every language.

[b]The translations have not been checked by native speakers.[/b] They use Blizzard's own terms where possible, but some wording may be clumsy or wrong. If something reads oddly in your language, please post a better text in the comments or on [url=https://github.com/SMoRG75/SMoRGsQoL/issues]GitHub[/url]. Corrections are very welcome.
